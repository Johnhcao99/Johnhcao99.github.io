# About Me

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Mathematics at the University of Southern California.

My research focuses on nonlinear partial differential equations arising in fluid mechanics, particularly compressible flows, free-boundary problems, and stability.

I am also interested in quantitative research, statistical learning, and scientific computing.

## Research Interests

- Compressible Navier–Stokes and Euler equations
- Free-boundary problems and physical vacuum
- Stability of expanding solutions
- Self-gravitating fluid models

## Publications and Preprints

### Published Articles

**Expanding Solutions to Free Boundary 3D Spherically Symmetric Compressible Navier–Stokes–Poisson Equations near the Lane–Emden Stars**  
Han Cao. *Journal of Differential Equations*, **478** (2026), Article 114572.  
[Journal](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jde.2026.114572) | [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.18339) | [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2511.18339)

Establishes global weak solutions under spherical symmetry and expansion results for strong solutions of viscous self-gravitating fluid models.

### Preprints

**Nonlinear Stability of Linearly Expanding Goldreich–Weber Solutions for the Navier–Stokes–Poisson System with Degenerate Viscosity Under Radial Perturbations**  
Han Cao. arXiv:2607.28960 (2026).  
[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28960) | [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.28960)

Proves nonlinear stability of linearly expanding Goldreich–Weber solutions under radial perturbations for a range of density-dependent shear viscosities with zero bulk viscosity.
