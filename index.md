# About Me

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Mathematics at the University of
Southern California.

My research focuses on nonlinear partial differential equations
arising in fluid mechanics, particularly compressible flows,
free-boundary problems, and stability.

I am also interested in quantitative research, statistical
learning, and scientific computing.

## Research Interests

- Compressible Navier–Stokes and Euler equations
- Free-boundary problems and physical vacuum
- Stability of expanding solutions
- Self-gravitating fluid models
